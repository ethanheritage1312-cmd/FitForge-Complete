Gym programmes

Explore Gym Training

These example workout structures can help you plan your sessions. Adjust exercises to your ability and equipment.

🌱
BEGINNER

Full-Body Foundations

Learn basic movement patterns and get comfortable with gym equipment.

  • Leg press or chair squat
  • Chest press machine
  • Seated row machine
  • Comfortable-paced cardio

Suggested schedule: 2–3 non-consecutive days weekly.

🏋️
GENERAL FITNESS

Strength and Fitness

Combine resistance exercises with cardiovascular activity.

  • Squat variation
  • Chest press or push-up
  • Lat pulldown or row
  • Walking, cycling or rowing

Focus: Good technique and gradual progress.

⚡
FLEXIBLE

Short Gym Sessions

A compact session for days when you have less time available.

  • Choose 3–4 familiar exercises
  • Warm up before working sets
  • Rest as needed between sets
  • Finish with optional easy cardio

Focus: Consistency, not rushing.

Home programmes

Make Your Home Your Training Space

Try these example routines with suitable modifications. Equipment is optional unless stated.

🏠
NO EQUIPMENT

Home Workout Basics

A simple introduction to exercising in your own space.

  • Chair squats
  • Wall push-ups
  • Glute bridges
  • Marching in place

Focus: Comfortable movement and control.

💪
OPTIONAL EQUIPMENT

Home Strength

Use bodyweight or suitable resistance equipment you already own.

  • Squat variation
  • Incline push-ups
  • Resistance-band rows
  • Glute bridges

Focus: Controlled repetitions and progression.

🚶
LOW-IMPACT OPTION

Move More at Home

A gentler option for building an exercise habit.

  • Marching in place
  • Side steps
  • Chair-supported movements
  • Gentle mobility exercises

Focus: Start at a comfortable pace.

Training note: These are general example routines, not personalised medical advice. Begin at a manageable level, use a clear and safe exercise area, and stop if an exercise causes pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms.
