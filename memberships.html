Membership FAQs

Some common questions about choosing a membership.

Which membership should I choose?

Consider your training preferences, the features you want and your budget. Compare the plans before making a choice.

Can beginners join FitForge?

The FitForge concept is designed to welcome different experience levels. Start with manageable activities and seek qualified guidance when needed.

Can I change my membership later?

Upgrade and cancellation arrangements have not yet been specified. Confirm the final membership terms before accepting payments or sign-ups.

How do I sign up?

Use the membership button to visit the joining page. The current website is a front-end concept and does not yet process registrations or payments.
