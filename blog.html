5 Tips for Getting Started

Simple principles that can help you build a sustainable routine.

1. Start at Your Own Pace

Choose manageable activities and build up gradually as your confidence and experience grow.

2. Learn Good Technique

Ask a qualified trainer for help with unfamiliar equipment and exercises.

3. Make a Realistic Plan

Choose days and activities that fit your everyday schedule instead of trying to do everything at once.

4. Practise Consistently

Regular practice and gradual progression are more sustainable than constantly changing your routine.

5. Allow Time for Recovery

Include rest, sufficient sleep and comfortable movement as part of a balanced routine.

Keep Your Approach Balanced

Eat a variety of foods, drink water regularly and seek qualified advice for individual nutrition needs.

Make Habits Easier to Maintain

Prepare your gym bag, schedule sessions and choose activities that make you feel comfortable.

Explore Cardio Activities

Walking, cycling, swimming and dancing are examples of activities you can adapt to your ability.

Ask for Guidance

A qualified fitness professional can help you choose suitable exercises and learn safe technique.
